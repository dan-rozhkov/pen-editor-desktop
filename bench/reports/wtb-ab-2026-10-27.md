User rule: even a light regression means a rollback.

## Decision rule (fixed before the start)

- Wait until all 10 apps finish in both arms, no early stop.
- Roll back ba084e5 if macro F1 of B is lower than that of A by 8 points or more.
- A smaller gap = "no harm found" (the measurement cannot tell it from noise).
- Not covered by this bench: native macOS app facts, and the "Clean up" step (every run starts with a fresh profile).

The arms were stopped after wtb-0013 (the 5th app) by the operator (`stop.sh`), then the benchmark was lost. The rule was not applied formally.

## Side observations

- The B drop is one event: the wtb-0011 timeout (A needed 1741 s, only 59 s under the cap). Without it B is not worse than A.
- B was about 11% slower on the 3 apps that finished in both arms, and made more main-chat requests (wtb-0003: 6 vs 12 main requests, 342 vs 453 subagent requests).
- B used `get_screenshot` (wtb-0007, 4 calls) and `trace_read` (wtb-0003, 1 call); A used neither. Both are tools, not the prompt part.
- Backend log in both arms: `skillRouting pass 1 failed: System One ... Missing Authentication header` (known, no auto-pick of skills; same in both).
- Backend log: `[browse trace] not saved: assets: invalid path "dom/0.txt"` (local desktop is 22 commits behind main). Does not affect scoring.
- A `rerun` of wtb-0011 x2 with a 45 min cap was prepared (`chain.sh`) and then replaced by a plain stop; it never ran.
